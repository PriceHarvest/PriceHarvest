How this prediction works

We trained machine-learning models on daily vegetable prices from the government Agmarknet portal. For each city and crop we automatically pick the model that performed best on unseen data, then use it to forecast the next 30 days.

Accuracy: on completely held-out test data the models achieve an average error of 22.4% (better than a strong baseline of 27%).

Forecasts are data-driven estimates, not guaranteed market prices. Prices are per Quintal (₹/kg = ÷100). The expected range is the model’s prediction ± its typical error for that series, measured on data it never saw during training.
